Back in my Nokia 1100 days (best phone ever made, by the way - doubled as a brick at home!), texting had a natural expressiveness that got lost somewhere along the way. Tone, in general, is hard to communicate in written language. You'd fire off a message like:

> hey :) saw ur msg late :P was sleeping.. call tomo tc ciao

Every emoticon did some heavy lifting, especially when the reader is not cognitively present enough to hear it right. It told the reader *how* to hear the sentence.
Without it, "saw ur msg late" can read curt or apologetic. With `:P` it reads playful, unbothered. The emotion glyphs did the work that vocal inflection does in speech.

I had this idea I called **Emoji Writing** - what if we brought that same practice into longer-form prose? Not the social media way, where emojis replace words or pile up at the end of a caption. More like how a screenplay has parentheticals *(sarcastically)*, *(beat)*, *(under her/his breath)* - except lighter, inline, and using the emoticon vocabulary we already share.

## Exempli gratia

Plain text:

> I spent three hours debugging a typo in a config file. The fix was changing a single character. I closed my laptop and went for a walk.

With emoji writing:

> I spent three hours debugging a typo in a config file :expressionless: The fix was changing a single character :man_facepalming: I closed my laptop and went for a walk :tired_face:

The emojis add the *tone* the words can't carry on their own. Less left to interpretation, which is not always a good thing, but better when you do it on purpose.
They can disambiguate:

- Was the walk angry or relieved?
- Was the debugging frustrating or funny in hindsight?

## My Emoji Vocabulary

These are the ones I reach for. Blog engines can render them natively from shortcodes, so this section doubles as a live test that they all work.

| Shortcode | Renders | General Usage |
|-----------|---------|---------------|
| `:smile:` | :smile: | Default positive, :) All good / that's fine |
| `:grin:` | :grin: | Genuine amusement :D |
| `:wink:` | :wink: | Playful, tongue-in-cheek |
| `:stuck_out_tongue:` | :stuck_out_tongue: | The classic `:P` - sarcasm lite |
| `:thinking:` | :thinking: | Genuinely unsure, or "hmm" |
| `:sweat:` | :sweat: | Uh-oh / Oh Boy! |
| `:expressionless:` | :expressionless: | Deadpan, the "really?" face |
| `:sweat_smile:` | :sweat_smile: | Awkward, self-aware |
| `:man_facepalming:` | :man_facepalming: | Should have known better |
| `:relieved:` | :relieved: | Weight off, exhale |
| `:joy:` | :joy: | Actually laughing |
| `:fire:` | :fire: | Emphasis, "this is good/intense" |
| `:rocket:` | :rocket: | Excitement, shipping energy |
| `:tada:` | :tada: | Celebration, milestone |
| `:thumbsup:` | :thumbsup: | Agreement, "noted" |
| `:eyes:` | :eyes: | "Look at this" / attention |
| `:100:` | :100: | Full agreement, nailed it |
