Over the past week, I finally started a couple of series I'd been meaning to watch for a while. And it hit me - this is how some semblance of a normal life sets in.

Before kids, my wife and I used to binge shows over our lunches and dinners, especially during the work-from-home COVID era. We were both at Amazon - same Slack, same comms, same rhythm. That was an interesting phase of life. Then I moved to Zillow, she moved to AWS, and a lot happened. Especially with two kids, you hardly get time for any reading or leisure. Watching TV series falls squarely on that line.

So the shows I suddenly started - because of this Apple TV subscription that comes with the SFR Reserve credit card, which makes no sense. We're basically paying for a service we don't want or use. But with it comes a better UI on the Fire Stick, and access to shows like:

- **Ted Lasso** - been meaning to get to this for a long time, thanks to John Green's recommendation
- **Severance** - the nerd community (Inbred and others) kept recommending it
- **Margot's Got Money Problems** - started this because Nick Offerman (Ron from Parks & Rec) is in it, alongside Michelle Pfeiffer and that Nordic actor whose name I'm blanking on

I watched the first episode of all three, and boy, do I miss intelligent people television. Something is to be said for high-quality entertainment content - the kind that respects the viewer's intelligence.
