Effective: November 13, 2026 (app version 0.26.0)

Logbook (package `com.kyubits.logbook`) is an Android app for logging personal metrics like weight, steps, and other health check-ins. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes.

**Short version:** Logbook has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. The app does not have internet permission, and the developer runs no server that receives your data. Everything you log stays on your phone.

Contact: **nssbusstob@gmail.com**

## What the app stores

Entries you type or speak (values, times, tags, notes, and the transcript of a spoken entry) and your settings, such as targets and reminders, are saved in a database on your phone. App backup is turned off, so this data is not copied to cloud backups. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Health Connect

If you allow it, Logbook uses Android Health Connect to:

- **read** weight, steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, and sleep, including past data and reads in the background, so they appear alongside your own entries;
- **write** weight and nutrition entries you log, so other health apps you choose can see them.

Data read from Health Connect is stored only on your phone and is used only to show your own trends in the app. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. You can change or remove these permissions at any time in Android's Health Connect settings.

Logbook's use of information received from Health Connect follows the [Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134), including its Limited Use requirements.

## Microphone (voice entries)

When you tap the mic, Logbook uses your phone's speech recognizer to turn what you say into text. That recognizer is provided by your phone, usually Google. It may process audio on its provider's servers under that provider's privacy policy ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). Logbook receives only the text and does not keep the audio.

## Sharing

When you use Share, the app makes an image of the current screen and hands it to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

## Notifications and reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder data leaves the device.

## Children

Logbook is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
